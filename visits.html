Educational visits

A real day of agriculture

Schools and colleges are welcome. Students join us in the paddy fields to harvest, thresh and winnow rice by hand — then see the orchard, herd and trout tanks.

  • Paddy harvesting
  • Threshing & winnowing
  • Orchard & herd tour
  • Student groups welcome

The day

What students do

  1. 1

    Harvest

    Cut ripened paddy by hand in the field.

  2. 2

    Thresh

    Separate the grain from the stalk the traditional way.

  3. 3

    Winnow

    Let the wind carry the husk away.

  4. 4

    Tour

    Walk the orchard, meet the herd and see the trout tanks.
