The farmstead

Four kinds of farming.
One honest table.

Welkin Farmstead is an agricultural and aquaculture farm rooted in Sopore — the apple town of Kashmir. We tend modern high-density orchards, raise livestock the traditional way, and, through Welkin Aquaculture, grow rainbow trout in clean recirculating tanks.

Everything we sell, we raise. That's the whole idea behind the name: goodness you can trace back to a field, a tree or a tank.

— Managed by Waseem Anayat Hajini

Welkin Aquaculture

Something fresh
is making waves.

We're bringing farm-fresh rainbow trout straight from our tanks in Model Town, Sopore to your kitchen. No long journeys, no mystery — just cold-water fish raised with care in a closed, clean loop.

  • Raised in a Recirculating Aquaculture System (RAS)
  • Water is filtered, balanced and re-oxygenated — then reused
  • Far less water than a traditional pond
  • Short farm-to-table journey
