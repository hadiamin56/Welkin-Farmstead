/ The farmstead

Welkin Farmstead is a working farm in Sopore, Kashmir. We grow apples and stone fruit in high-density orchards, raise rainbow trout in recirculating tanks, keep sheep, bulls and milk animals, and open our paddy fields to students. Everything we sell, we raise ourselves.

Managed by Waseem Anayat Hajini
