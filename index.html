Our farm

Four ways we farm

High-density orchards

Closely planted, trellised trees give earlier fruit and easier picking. We grow apples, pears, Ruby Nectarines, apricots, cherries (Premium, Cordia, Regina), kiwi and walnuts — and raise vegetable and flower seedlings.

  • Apples & pears
  • Cherries, apricots, nectarines
  • Kiwi & walnuts
  • Seedlings for your garden

Welkin Aquaculture

Rainbow trout raised in a Recirculating Aquaculture System, now expanding in Model Town, Sopore. Water is filtered, balanced and re-oxygenated, then reused — so the fish stay healthy and the journey to your kitchen stays short.

  • Clean, controlled tanks
  • Uses far less water than open ponds
  • Fresh trout, straight from the farm

Livestock & dairy

Fresh milk, bulls and sheep — including Dumba and Merino. Eid-ul-Adha Qurbani spots fill early, so we take bookings ahead of time.

Paddy fields & learning

Students join us in the paddy fields to harvest, thresh and winnow rice by hand — a day of real agriculture, not a textbook.

  • Paddy harvesting
  • Threshing & winnowing
