The Welkin journey

From the first tree to the table

Six chapters of one farm growing in Sopore.

  1. Chapter 1

    The first trees

    It began with high-density apple orchards in Sopore — closely planted rows built for early, generous fruit.

  2. Chapter 2

    More to harvest

    Pears, Ruby Nectarines, apricots, three cherry varieties, kiwi and walnuts joined the apples.

  3. Chapter 3

    A herd on the green

    Dumba and Merino sheep, bulls and milk — the farm's animals, raised on open pasture.

  4. Chapter 4

    Welkin Aquaculture

    Rainbow trout in clean recirculating tanks, now expanding in Model Town, Sopore.

  5. Chapter 5

    Fields that teach

    Students arrive to harvest, thresh and winnow — the farm becomes a classroom.

  6. Chapter 6

    Growing together

    A growing team, Qurbani bookings, fresh orders — and the next chapter still to be written.
