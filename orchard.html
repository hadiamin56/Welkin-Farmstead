Why high-density

More trees, earlier fruit

In a high-density orchard, trees are planted close together and trained on supports. That means more fruiting trees per acre, fruit that starts earlier, and care and picking that are easier on the people doing it.

  • More trees per acre
  • Earlier fruit
  • Easier care & picking
  • Seedlings for your garden too
